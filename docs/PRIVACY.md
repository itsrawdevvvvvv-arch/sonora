# Privacy

Sonora is built to need as little as possible.

- **No network permission.** The app cannot access the internet. It never
  uploads, syncs or phones home.
- **No account.** There is nothing to sign in to.
- **No analytics, no ads, no trackers.** The APK contains no third-party code.
- **Your music stays put.** Sonora reads your device's audio library through
  Android's `MediaStore` and plays it locally. Nothing is copied off the device.

The only permission Sonora requests is read access to your audio files, so it
can list and play them. You can revoke it at any time in Android Settings.
